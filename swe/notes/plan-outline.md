# Plan outline (input for task-file writers; PLAN.md is the index)

Sprint/task ids: `S###/T###`. Deps are listed by id. Every task must leave `cargo xtask ci` green.
Spec paths are relative to `swe/specs/`. pi-studio fixtures: `../pi-studio/packages/protocol/fixtures/external-clients/` at pi-studio commit `47d80e6`.
